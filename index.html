Meet Dr. Baishali Bhattacharjee

Dr. Baishali Bhattacharjee is a dedicated homeopathic physician based in Guwahati, Assam, with extensive experience in classical homeopathy. She is known for her thorough case-taking, empathetic listening, and deeply individualised approach to treatment.
